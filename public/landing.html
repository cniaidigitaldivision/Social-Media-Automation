Everything between the idea and the post

Six parts of the pipeline, built for teams that publish across every brand they run.

Smart Composer

Write the post once, then customise the caption for each platform without leaving the editor. A live preview renders your post exactly as each network will show it, and media validation catches the rules before you schedule. Instagram, TikTok and YouTube each require a video or image, and YouTube will not accept a post without a Title.

Team Approvals

Every post passes human review before it can go out. Reviewers work from a single queue where they can edit captions per platform, swap the attached media, and approve or reject without opening the composer. Nothing reaches a live account that a person has not signed off on.

Visual Calendar

A colour-coded month view of every scheduled, published and failed post across all of your workspaces at once. Click any entry to open its full detail, and approve or reject it inline without breaking your place in the calendar.

Secure Token Vault

All OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM. They are never stored in the automation layer. The publishing engine fetches them securely at publish time only, so a credential never sits idle where it does not need to be.

Automated Publishing

An n8n-powered engine checks every minute for posts that have come due and publishes them to Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok and YouTube. Each platform gets its own logic, from Instagram’s two-step container flow to YouTube’s resumable upload, so the quirks are handled for you.

Multi-Workspace

Run every business brand in its own workspace, each with its own connected accounts and content pipeline. A per-workspace brand kit holds the voice, tone, hashtags, banned words and CTA library, so the right rules follow the right brand automatically.
